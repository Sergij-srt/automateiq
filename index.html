Inquiries

Frequently Answered Questions

How do you handle data access and privacy?

We agree on access, hosting, and data handling before work begins. The setup depends on the tools you use and your security requirements; where practical, integrations can run in accounts and infrastructure you control.

Who pays for AI and platform usage?

Usage fees depend on the providers, models, and volume in your workflow. We can connect tools to accounts you control and review expected costs while scoping the work.

What happens if a third-party API (like HubSpot or Simpro) goes down?

We can add validation, retries, alerts, and a human review step where they fit the process. The exact handling depends on the systems and the cost of an error or delay.

Do we get full ownership of the code and workflows?

Deliverables, access, and handover are agreed in the project scope. We document the workflow and provide the files and account access covered by the contract.
